Privacy policy: Neuron Island uploader

Last updated 28 October 2026.

The Neuron Island uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Neuron Island YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

  • YouTube data: it uploads videos, thumbnails and descriptions to the owner's own channel, sets their publish schedule, and reads that channel's statistics and retention analytics.
  • Google Drive data: it creates and reads only files it uploads itself (drive.file scope), to archive the owner's video project files.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Credentials are stored only on the owner's computer.
